# GoHighLevel Data Processing Agreement

## Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between ZeroMotion Marketing ("Controller" or "we") and GoHighLevel ("Processor") for the processing of personal data in connection with our digital marketing services.

**Effective Date**: {new Date().toISOString().slice(0, 10)}
**Controller**: ZeroMotion Marketing LLC
**Processor**: GoHighLevel Inc.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data
- **"Controller"** means the entity determining the purposes and means of Processing
- **"Processor"** means the entity Processing Personal Data on behalf of the Controller
- **"Data Subject"** means the individual to whom Personal Data relates

## 2. Scope and Purpose of Processing

### 2.1 Categories of Data Subjects
- Website visitors and leads
- Existing clients and customers
- Marketing campaign recipients
- Support and service users

### 2.2 Categories of Personal Data
- Contact information (name, email, phone)
- Business information (company, industry, role)
- Communication data (emails, messages, chat logs)
- Behavioral data (website interactions, campaign engagement)
- Technical data (IP addresses, device information)

### 2.3 Processing Activities
- Data collection through website forms
- CRM data management and storage
- Marketing automation and email campaigns
- Analytics and performance tracking
- Customer support and communication

### 2.4 Purpose of Processing
To provide digital marketing services, CRM management, and marketing automation to our clients while ensuring compliance with applicable data protection laws.

## 3. Compliance with Laws

### 3.1 Applicable Laws
The Processor shall comply with all applicable data protection laws including:
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- CAN-SPAM Act
- Other applicable privacy and data protection laws

### 3.2 Data Protection Impact Assessment
Both parties agree to cooperate in conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments where required by applicable law.

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Security Measures
The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits and testing
- Incident response and breach notification procedures
- Regular backups and disaster recovery

### 4.2 Security Incident Notification
The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay (within 24 hours) of any security incident affecting Personal Data.

### 4.3 Security Audits
The Processor shall allow the Controller to conduct security audits of the Processing activities, subject to reasonable confidentiality obligations.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

### 5.1 Rights Implementation
The Processor shall assist the Controller in fulfilling Data Subject rights including:
- Right to access Personal Data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to restriction of processing

### 5.2 Response Time
The Processor shall respond to Data Subject requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

## 6. Sub-Processing

### 6.1 Authorized Sub-Processors
The Processor may engage sub-processors, provided that:
- The Controller is provided with a list of sub-processors
- The Processor enters into written agreements with sub-processors
- Sub-processors provide adequate data protection guarantees

### 6.2 Current Sub-Processors
GoHighLevel utilizes the following sub-processors:
- Amazon Web Services (cloud infrastructure)
- SendGrid (email delivery)
- Twilio (SMS and communication services)
- Stripe (payment processing)
- Other service providers as necessary

### 6.3 Sub-Processor Changes
The Processor shall provide the Controller with 30 days' notice of any changes to the list of sub-processors.

## 7. International Data Transfers

### 7.1 Transfer Mechanisms
Where Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the Processor shall ensure appropriate safeguards including:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)
- Adequacy decisions by relevant authorities

### 7.2 Transfer Impact Assessment
Both parties shall cooperate in conducting transfer impact assessments where required.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

### 8.1 Retention Periods
Personal Data shall be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this DPA.

### 8.2 Deletion Obligations
The Processor shall delete or return all Personal Data to the Controller upon termination of the services, unless retention is required by applicable law.

### 8.3 Data Backup
During the retention period, the Processor may retain backup copies of Personal Data for disaster recovery purposes, subject to the security measures in this DPA.

## 9. Audit and Compliance

### 9.1 Audit Rights
The Controller shall have the right to audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA, including:
- Annual security assessments
- Access to audit reports and certifications
- On-site audits with reasonable notice

### 9.2 Audit Costs
The Processor shall bear the costs of providing audit reports and certifications. The Controller shall bear the costs of any additional audits it requests.

## 10. Data Breach Notification

### 10.1 Notification Requirements
In the event of a Personal Data breach, the Processor shall:
- Notify the Controller without undue delay
- Provide sufficient information to allow the Controller to meet its notification obligations
- Cooperate with the Controller in investigating and mitigating the breach

### 10.2 Notification Timeline
- High-risk breaches: Within 24 hours
- Other breaches: Within 48 hours
- Regulatory notifications: As required by applicable law

## 11. Liability and Indemnification

### 11.1 Processor Liability
The Processor's liability for breaches of this DPA shall be limited in accordance with the main Terms of Service.

### 11.2 Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify the other against losses arising from its breach of this DPA, subject to the limitations in the main agreement.

## 12. Termination

### 12.1 Termination Rights
Either party may terminate this DPA if the other party materially breaches its obligations under this DPA.

### 12.2 Data Return or Deletion
Upon termination, the Processor shall return or delete all Personal Data in accordance with the Controller's instructions.

## 13. Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the laws of the State of Louisiana, United States, without regard to conflict of law principles.

## 14. Severability

If any provision of this DPA is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

## 15. Amendments

This DPA may only be amended in writing and signed by both parties.

# ZeroMotion Custom Website Build Pricing Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document outlines ZeroMotion's competitive pricing strategy for custom website builds, clearly differentiating from our existing CRM bundle offerings. The strategy positions us competitively in the market while ensuring profitability and clear value proposition for clients.

## Market Research Summary

Based on 2024 market analysis:
- Custom small business websites range from $3,000-$15,000 depending on complexity
- Freelance developers charge $50-150/hour for custom development
- Average project timeline: 2-6 weeks depending on scope
- Premium developers focusing on modern frameworks (Astro, Next.js) command higher rates

## Pricing Structure

### **Tier 1: Essential Custom Build - $3,500**
**Target:** Small businesses, personal brands, portfolio sites
**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks
**Features:**
- 3-5 page custom-coded website (Astro/HTML/CSS/JS)
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Basic SEO optimization & Google Analytics setup
- Contact forms & lead capture integration
- Performance optimization (90+ Lighthouse scores)
- 30 days of post-launch support
- Basic deployment & hosting setup guidance

**Hosting:** Client choice (we provide recommendations, setup assistance available for $20-50/mo)

### **Tier 2: Professional Custom Build - $5,500** ⭐ *Most Popular*
**Target:** Growing businesses, e-commerce, service companies
**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks
**Features:**
- 5-10 page custom-coded website (Astro/Next.js/Tailwind)
- Advanced animations & micro-interactions
- Comprehensive SEO optimization & schema markup
- E-commerce integration (if needed)
- Content Management System integration
- Advanced analytics & conversion tracking
- 60 days of post-launch support
- Performance optimization & caching strategies
- Optional CRM integration (+$500)

**Hosting:** Managed hosting included for first 6 months, then client choice

### **Tier 3: Premium Custom Build - $8,500+**
**Target:** Established brands, complex requirements, multi-location businesses
**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks
**Features:**
- 10+ page custom web application (Next.js/React/TypeScript)
- Complex functionality & third-party integrations
- Advanced performance optimization & CDN setup
- Multi-language support (if needed)
- Advanced analytics dashboard & conversion tracking
- Custom admin panel for content management
- 90 days of post-launch support
- CRM & marketing automation integration included
- Priority maintenance plan (3 months included)
- Advanced security implementations

**Hosting:** Fully managed hosting included for 1 year

## Differentiation from CRM Bundles

### **Starter CRM Websites ($200 + $75/mo)**
- **Built inside ZeroMotion CRM (GoHighLevel)**
- **Subscription model** - ongoing monthly fee
- **Managed service** - we handle everything
- **CRM & automation included by default**
- **Template-based with customization**
- **Best for:** Quick launch, all-in-one solution, budget-conscious

### **Custom Website Builds ($3,500-$8,500+)**
- **Hand-coded with modern frameworks**
- **Project-based model** - one-time payment
- **Client ownership** - you own the code and assets
- **CRM integration optional** - never required
- **Built from scratch** - no templates
- **Best for:** Full ownership, unique requirements, growth-focused brands
